# GTA DHAKA: City of Chaos — Special Operations & Side Activities Guide

This guide covers **Special Operations HQ**, all **5 Special Operations Missions**, all **8 Side Activities**, **Squad Companion Commands**, **Tactical Subdue**, and **Ranking / Unlockable Rewards**.

---

## 1. Special Operations HQ Location & Facilities

- **Map Location:** South-East Gulshan Command District — Entrance Door at **`(38.0, 46.8)`** (cyan `HQ` marker on MiniMap & Full Map).
- **Outdoor Tactical Bay:** **`(49.5, 42.0)`** — An unlocked **Spec Ops Tactical SUV** is parked outside for immediate deployment.
- **Stationed Duty Officer:** *Capt. Imran* stands outside at `(35.8, 48.0)`.
- **Inside the 3D Walk-In Interior (Press `F` / `Enter` at the door):**
  - **Operations Control Room Terminal** *(Left Wing: `-6.8, -2.4`)* — Open briefings, check prerequisites/ranks, choose difficulty (`Easy` / `Normal` / `Hard`), and deploy on any of the 5 Special Operations.
  - **Tactical Armory** *(Right Wing: `6.8, -2.4`)* — Live try-on and equip tactical uniforms:
    1. *Dhaka Rapid Response Suit* (Unlocked by default)
    2. *City Shield Field Medic* (Unlocked by default)
    3. *Platinum Ops Commander* (Unlocked upon completing **Op 05: Operation Final Resolve**)
  - **Central Holographic Briefing Table, Reception Desk, Indoor Vehicle Bay & Mission Results Board.**

> **Quick Access:** You can also launch Special Operations from **DhakaOS Smartphone (`T`) → Ops** or **Missions (`J`) → Special Ops**.

## 2. Squad Companion AI & Tactical Controls

During Special Operations (`deploySquad: true`), your 3-member squad deploys automatically:
- **Sgt. Hasan** — Tactical assault & perimeter security
- **Cpl. Rafi** — Flank support & objective push
- **Medic Nabila** — Automatic field healing when player or squad health drops

### Squad Commands (Floating Squad Bar or Keyboard `1`–`5`)
| Key | Command | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | **Follow** | Squad stays in tight formation behind you and boards/exits vehicles automatically with you. |
| `2` | **Hold** | Squad holds current defensive ground and engages nearby hostiles. |
| `3` | **Objective** | Squad advances toward the active mission waypoint or hostile cluster. |
| `4` | **Regroup** | Immediately recalls all squad members to your position (also triggers stuck recovery if needed). |
| `5` | **Protect** | Squad prioritizes shielding nearby escorted civilians/VIPs from hostiles. |

### Tactical Subdue (`R` or Touch `Subdue Hostile` Button)
- Approach any hostile gang member (crimson blip on minimap; gold blip for Boss *Raza*) and press **`R`** (or tap **`Subdue Hostile`**) to deal focused tactical takedown damage while your squad provides cover fire.
- **Civilian Protection Rule:** Harming civilians is never rewarded. If all protected civilians/VIPs are lost during an escort/rescue stage, the operation immediately fails.

## 3. The 5 Special Operations Missions

All missions feature fictional organizations (*Iron Syndicate* and *Black Viper Crew*):

### Op 01: Operation Safe Passage (`specops_01_safe_passage`)
- **Giver:** Major Tariq · Special Ops HQ
- **Base Reward:** `৳ 7,500` · **Time Limit:** `210s` (Normal)
- **Bonus Objective:** Keep all 3 civilians unharmed and take under 25 HP damage.
- **Stages:**
  1. Deploy to Gulshan West Plaza `(-54, 12)`.
  2. Neutralize **4 Iron Syndicate hostiles** threatening **3 civilians** at `(-54, 12)`.
  3. Escort all rescued civilians to the Hatirjheel Extraction Zone `(-18, 58)`.

### Op 02: Operation Silent Rescue (`specops_02_silent_rescue`)
- **Giver:** Inspector Selim · Intel Desk
- **Base Reward:** `৳ 8,500` · **Time Limit:** `230s` (Normal)
- **Bonus Objective:** Inspect clues and extract hostage with zero civilian harm.
- **Stages:**
  1. Inspect **2 clues** under Mohakhali Flyover: *Encrypted Burner Phone* `(-14, -54)` and *Warehouse Route Note* `(10, -58)`.
  2. Neutralize **4 Black Viper captors** and free hostage *Dr. Farhan* at North-West Block `(-58, -44)`.
  3. Escort the rescued hostage safely to Special Ops HQ `(38, 54)`.

### Op 03: Operation City Shield (`specops_03_city_shield`)
- **Giver:** Dhaka Emergency Command
- **Base Reward:** `৳ 9,500` · **Time Limit:** `215s` (Normal)
- **Bonus Objective:** Finish all 3 incidents with over 40 seconds remaining.
- **Police Support:** Active at all 3 sites.
- **Stages:**
  1. Secure Gulshan Circle `(0, 2)` — 3 hostiles, 2 civilians.
  2. Shield Gulshan Care Hospital Road `(-46, 4)` — 3 hostiles, 2 civilians.
  3. Clear Hatirjheel Promenade `(8, 60)` — 4 hostiles, 2 civilians.

### Op 04: Operation VIP Escort (`specops_04_vip_escort`)
- **Giver:** Major Tariq · Special Ops HQ
- **Base Reward:** `৳ 9,200` · **Time Limit:** `210s` (Normal)
- **Bonus Objective:** Keep VIP Commissioner Chowdhury completely unharmed.
- **Stages:**
  1. Meet VIP Commissioner outside Hotel Gulshan Grand `(44, -8)`.
  2. Board any escort vehicle with VIP & Squad.
  3. Repel **4 Iron Syndicate ambushers** near East Flyover Ramp `(54, -52)`.
  4. Deliver VIP safely to Special Ops HQ `(38, 54)`.

### Op 05: Operation Final Resolve (`specops_05_final_resolve`)
- **Prerequisite:** Complete **Op 01** (`specops_01_safe_passage`) and **Op 02** (`specops_02_silent_rescue`).
- **Giver:** Commander General Alam · HQ
- **Base Reward:** `৳ 15,000` · **Time Limit:** `260s` (Normal)
- **Bonus Objective:** Complete with Squad intact and zero civilian harm for Platinum Rank.
- **Finale First-Clear Unlocks:**
  - **Platinum Ops Commander Uniform** (equippable in HQ Armory)
  - **Tactical Interceptor SUV** (added to your owned garage & summonable via DhakaOS Ride)
- **Stages:**
  1. Inspect final clue (*Syndicate Command Tablet*) near Digital Market Plaza `(16, 28)`.
  2. Protect 2 civilians and confront **5 hostiles including Boss Raza** at Hatirjheel East `(58, 56)` with Police Support.
  3. Return to Special Operations HQ `(38, 52)` for Mission Debrief.

## 5. Ranking Formula & Replay Rules

- **BRONZE:** Completed, or any protected civilian took harm.
- **SILVER:** Clean completion with zero civilian harm.
- **GOLD:** Zero civilian harm + low player damage (`<= 20 HP`) or optional bonus / fast finish.
- **PLATINUM:** Zero civilian harm + low damage + optional bonus met (extra boost for fast finish with `>= 28%` time remaining and/or `HARD` difficulty).
- **Replay Protection:** First-clear bonus and special finale unlocks trigger once per mission; replays remain available for improving best rank/time and earning scaled Taka rewards.
